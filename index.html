Methodology & data sources

Ballots, not candidate votes. Texas does not register voters by political party. The Secretary of State's early-vote turnout reports do not provide an official Democratic/Republican/independent voter breakdown. Until a documented, licensed model is supplied, party and shift metrics display Unavailable.

Historical alignment. Comparisons use the same ordinal early-voting day. 2018 daily data has narrower county coverage than 2022/2024/2026; this site will not compare unmatched county populations as though they were equivalent. An alternative days-before-election comparison can be added later.

Daily additions. Calculated as differences in consecutive cumulative reports for the same year and coverage. They may reflect corrections, late county reporting, and mail-ballot updates rather than votes physically cast that day.

Data status. This tracker includes a verified 254-county October 23, 2026 snapshot for the interactive map. Additional statewide timeline reports must be independently validated before publication. The dashboard reads it automatically. Each record includes its source URL and coverage label.
